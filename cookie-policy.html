Cookie Policy

Effective date: 27 May 2026

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on the Area 67 Barbershop website.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and provide insights into usage.

2. Cookie Types We Use

  • Essential cookies: required for core site functionality and security.
  • Preference cookies: remember user choices such as cookie consent preferences.
  • Analytics cookies: optional cookies used to understand site performance and user behavior trends.

3. Third-Party Services

Our booking flow is delivered through a third-party booking provider. Their domain may set cookies according to their own privacy and cookie documentation when you visit their booking page.

4. Managing Consent

You can choose between Accept All and Essential Only in the cookie banner. You can reopen these controls any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer.

5. Browser Controls

You can also block or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect basic site functionality.

6. Contact

Questions about cookie usage can be sent to hello@area67.ie.
