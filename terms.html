Booking Terms

Effective date: 27 May 2026

These terms apply to all appointments booked with Area 67 Barbershop.

1. Appointments

  • Appointments are confirmed once successfully completed through our booking platform.
  • Please arrive on time. Late arrival may reduce service time or require rescheduling.

2. Pricing

  • Prices are displayed in EUR.
  • Pricing may vary by barber and service length.
  • Out-of-hours services have separate rates.

3. Student and Child Services

  • Student rates require a valid student ID at appointment time.
  • Child appointments may require parent or guardian attendance depending on age.

4. Cancellations and No-Shows

  • Please cancel or reschedule with as much notice as possible.
  • Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may require prepayment for future bookings.

5. Refunds

  • If a payment issue occurs, contact us promptly to review a fair resolution.
  • Completed grooming services are generally non-refundable unless required by law.

6. Conduct and Safety

  • We reserve the right to refuse service in cases of abusive, unsafe, or inappropriate behavior.
  • Clients must disclose relevant scalp or skin conditions that may affect service delivery.

7. Contact

Questions regarding bookings or these terms can be sent to hello@area67.ie.

Postal address: Area67 Barbershop, Wentworth Place, Wicklow, Ireland A67 XT71.
