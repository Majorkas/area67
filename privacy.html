Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 May 2026

Area 67 Barbershop is committed to protecting your personal data in line with the GDPR and Irish data protection law.

1. Who We Are

Data Controller: Area67 Barbershop, Wentworth Place, Wicklow, Ireland A67 XT71.

Contact: hello@area67.ie

2. Data We Collect

  • Booking details such as name, contact details, selected service, barber, and appointment time.
  • Service history and notes needed to deliver your requested grooming service.
  • Website technical data such as IP address, browser, and device metadata through essential site cookies.

3. Why We Use Your Data

  • To provide and manage appointments.
  • To communicate booking confirmations, reminders, and service updates.
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations.
  • To improve website performance where optional analytics consent is given.

4. Legal Bases

  • Performance of a contract for appointment and service delivery.
  • Legitimate interests for running and improving our service.
  • Consent for optional analytics and non-essential cookies.
  • Legal obligation where records are required by law.

5. Third-Party Processors

We use a third-party booking platform (Resurva) to process appointments. Their platform handles part of the personal data required to complete booking and account access.

6. Retention

Booking and service records are retained only for as long as necessary for business operations, legal compliance, and dispute handling, then securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Object to certain processing and request restriction.
  • Withdraw consent for optional cookie processing at any time.

8. Contact and Complaints

To exercise your rights, contact hello@area67.ie. You may also contact the Irish Data Protection Commission if you are dissatisfied with our response.

This policy should be reviewed with legal counsel before production launch, especially if payment processing, marketing automation, or additional data sources are introduced.
