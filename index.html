Who We Are

Mark Quinn, owner of Area67 Barbershop, picked up his first set of clippers in 2016, driven by passion and a clear vision. What started as a dream became a journey of dedication, long hours, and mastering the craft of barbering. Every cut, every client, and every lesson learned shaped the path forward.

Quinn's Cuts might not have reached the success Mark wanted, but the mistakes made along the way became vital when building AREA67 into what it is today.

Fast forward to 2023 and Area67 was born. Not just a barbershop, but a community. A place where style meets culture. Mark's vision became reality, transforming how people experience a haircut.

Now it is 2026. Area67 is more than a business; it is a movement. A testament to hard work, perseverance, and believing in your dreams. From day one to today and far beyond, this is only the beginning.
